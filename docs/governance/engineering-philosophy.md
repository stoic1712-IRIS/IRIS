# Engineering Philosophy

**Status:** Canonical

**Version:** 1.0.0

STOIC-IRIS engineering follows these permanent principles:

1. Governance precedes autonomy and implementation.
2. Understanding, observability, and repairability constrain complexity.
3. Every capability must unlock or permanently support later capabilities.
4. Shared contracts precede parallel services.
5. External technologies are evaluated as replaceable providers, not adopted as sovereign owners.
6. Evidence, rollback, cleanup, security, licensing, and documentation are part of implementation.
7. Read-only intent remains read-only unless change authority is explicit.
8. Least privilege and task-scoped context apply to every worker and tool.
9. Deterministic verification is preferred; material output requires independent review.
10. Failures remain visible and repairs preserve provenance.
11. IRIS Core and Layer 4 products remain separate.
12. Repeated manual work becomes an automation candidate only after the manual process is governed and understood.
13. Dependencies are minimized, pinned, reviewed, replaceable, and removable.
14. Completion means the applicable gate is proven, not that machinery or a demonstration exists.

Engineering decisions optimize for sovereignty, safety, correctness, maintainability, portability, and compounding capability before novelty or speed.
